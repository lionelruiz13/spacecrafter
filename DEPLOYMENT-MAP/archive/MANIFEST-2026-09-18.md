## Why WHOLE-FILE and not per-id (a supervisor choice, a veto point)

The predecessor's head and closing critical path entangle several ids per sentence — there is no
byte-exact per-id unit to move. A whole-file copy also keeps every historical
`DEPLOYMENT-MAP.md:<line>` citation of its last state resolvable: `:383-398`, `:635`, `:99` and
the rest all still land on the text that was there when they were written.

**From the new form on, the convention is per-id:** a closed item leaves the live surface as a
pure move to `DEPLOYMENT-MAP/archive/<id>.md` (ids `R<n>`, `T1.<n>`, `T5.<n>`, or the ledger id
for the T2/T3/T4 tiers), with this manifest gaining a row. Ids are never renumbered and never
reused; the gap an archived id leaves in a live list IS the marker.
