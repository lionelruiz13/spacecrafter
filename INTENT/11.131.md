    - **(c) SKY LOCK — the channel is REAL, it is shipped, and the defect is §11.129's one layer up.** `[observed: core.cpp; measured: same harness]` `Core::setFlagLockSkyPosition` is dual (§11.58) — but **four sites write the old flag alone**: `selectObject` and `selectType` turn it on when an object is selected while tracking (*"keep the earth following"*), and `autoZoomOut` turns it off twice on the zoom-out-to-init path.
