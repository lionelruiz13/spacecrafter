## (c) What the campaign settles about the instrument itself

- The record is reproducible run to run at the floor: rest-phase values on the same pin agree bit-for-bit between the two launches of each pin on every body (the uniform offset is identical to 9 digits on both pins); the engine-time alignment pairs every frame (0 unpaired in every phase).
- Three reader limits, each found by a delta that was the reader's and not the engine's (F164): (a) under running time the comparison must align on JD (elapsed engine time differs by the dt jitter between runs, 2 ms = 0.7 deg of sky at 86400x); (b) the k x median JUMP rule has no floor: a body whose median step is 0 flags every sub-ulp step (`inf`), so `motion`'s flag is unreadable on near-still bodies - a floor at the record's own precision (1e-6 r) and the median over non-zero steps; **[the median over non-zero steps was itself measured to hide a single jump of an otherwise still body (11.339 (i) C1); the rule landed is the SPIKE: a step above the floor and above k x the larger neighbouring step]** (c) a command boundary puts the two runs one frame apart (the script's `wait` lands on a frame edge differently per run): the run-against-run worst row of a phase is that frame's, so the reader should also report the delta minimized over a +-1 frame window beside the strict one.
- The supervisor's own digest misread two exponents (`5.33078793e-07` cut to ten characters) and nearly classified the old engine's rest as jumping: the correction was the raw rows (`screen_record.load`), the lesson Q-67's - a number is read from the instrument's full print, never from a truncated view.
