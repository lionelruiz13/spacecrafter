    - **(d) What the newly-running destructors actually touch, checked at source before the runs [observed]** — the prediction the measurements then confirmed. Order is safe by member declaration, not by luck: `App` declares `Context context;` BEFORE `std::shared_ptr<Core> core`, so reverse-order member teardown destroys the body tree FIRST, while the managers live (`allocSet` states the same invariant from the other side: *"pools are registry-lifetime, which outlives every module"*), and `~App` runs `VulkanMgr::waitIdle()` before `core.reset()`.
