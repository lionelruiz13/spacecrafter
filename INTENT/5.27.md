    - **MECHANISM CORRECTED 2026-07-24 (§11.101(f)) — the defect and its ~7000 km magnitude stand, its cause and D21's option set do NOT.** Grounded children DO read the scaled datum — **once, at LOAD, and bake it** (`orbitModules/SurfacePointOrbitLoader.hpp:88` takes `parent->getAltitudeReference()` = `scaledDatumRadius`, `:112` folds it into `const double altStart` and replays it every frame) while the observer's own reference is recomputed whenever `uncached` and `scaling` is a **5-second `ASmooth` ramp** applied AFTER the system loads. So today's behaviour is neither "displayed" nor "physical" but **HISTORICAL** — whatever scaling was live at load time — with three consequences: (i) **both D21 options require a change and neither can be chosen "because it is today's behaviour"**; (ii) it is the 4th instance of the closed load-time-latch class (B15 AoI / B19 position / B32 spin), so the model-layer half may need no new decision at all; (iii) the leak runs the WRONG way — `ModularBody.hpp:193` declares *"Just visual scaling"* yet a display flag sets a PHYSICAL position a script can fetch (a D8 violation on the script channel). Scope is broader than the title: `planet_scale` and the sun path reach the same latch (`ssystem_factory.hpp:556/240/267`). Arithmetic re-derived: 1737.4 × (5−1) = **6949.6 km** vs the recorded ~7000. Untested timing prediction: startup-loaded and reload-loaded scenes latch different values across the ramp ⇒ **`body action reload` should MOVE a composed rover.**
    - **BEHAVIOUR HALF UNBLOCKED 2026-08-26 — D21 ANSWERED** [vixy 2026-08-22, harness `d521807a`; propagated **§11.149(c)**; fix rides task **F39**]. Verbatim: *"Unscaled for physics (orbit, shadow received and casted), scaled for bounding/rendering, grounded children inherit scaling (to be visually identical to unscaled, given grounded bodies are surface-relative, so the referential is the body surface)"* — §2(a)'s two-layer rule, **confirming** (not merely permitting) §11.101(f)'s derived placement: the model layer places from the UNSCALED datum, the riding-of-the-displayed-surface lives at the presentation layer. Three readings the fix must carry, each with its veto point at §11.149(c):
      - **(i) the load-time latch dies by implication, and the implication is forced** [derived] — *"inherit"* names a LIVE relation and a baked `const double` cannot inherit a 5 s ramp. Veto only if a load-time copy was meant, which the ramp makes observably wrong; the `body action reload` prediction above becomes F39's **pre-fix discriminator** and then its **post-fix invariant**.
      - **(ii) *"to be visually identical to unscaled"* is an ACCEPTANCE CRITERION, not a description** [derived, veto] — read with *"the referential is the body surface"*: the scaling is a **uniform dilation of the parent's surface neighbourhood — child placement AND extent** — so the composition is self-similar. **The rival reading is the veto point**: if only the RADIAL PLACEMENT inherits while the child keeps its true size, a 3 m rover on a `moon_scale=5` Moon looks five times smaller against the ground than unscaled — which is what *"visually identical to unscaled"* appears to exclude. The two differ observably; the gate is the criterion, not placement alone.
      - **(iii) *"scaled for bounding"* does NOT decide the REACH half of `boundingRadius`** [derived, flagged not decided] — on this path `boundingRadius` is both culling extent and navigational reach, and that coupling is §11.96(e)'s promotion-grade item, suspended for Vixy. F39 records and suspends if forced; it does not settle it in passing.
      Also settled by the answer's own words: **shadow cast AND received read UNSCALED radii** (magnitudes measured before/after, corrected where they read scaled today); **bounding stays SCALED**; and `ModularBody.hpp:193`'s contract becomes TRUE — a script-fetched grounded position stops depending on `flag moon_scaled`, closing the D8 script-channel violation of (iii) above. Unblocked with it: **B31's T3** and the retirement-or-argument of the harness-wide `flag moon_scaled off` workaround (`b24_screen` and every grounded screen harness).
