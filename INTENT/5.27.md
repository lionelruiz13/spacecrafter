# INTENT §5.27

27. **Grounded children don't ride parent display scaling** — [defect, found 2026-07-24 by the F1/B3 root-cause chain, §11.100; OPEN, behavior half gated on D21]
    - Observable [measured]: with the shipped default `flag_moon_scaled=true, moon_scale=5`, a composed grounded rover on the Moon is INVISIBLE at every observer distance — its placement uses the real `datum_radius` (1737 km) while the Moon draws at `scaledRadius` (8687 km): the child sits ~7000 km under the displayed surface, correctly occluded (far) or lost in the scaled-surface regime hole (close, §11.97(e)). Causal lever: `flag moon_scaled off` ⇒ the same rover renders with working occlusion (68537 px vs 0, §11.100(b)).
    - Root [observed at source]: `updateCache` scales the body's draw radius AND `scaledDatumRadius`, but the grounded-child fold places children from the unscaled surface geometry — the display transform is applied to the parent's surface and not to what stands on it.
    - Consequence: the §11.78(a) composition-mandate scenes ("rover on a moon") are broken under the default planetarium config; every screen harness for grounded content must set `flag moon_scaled off` until resolved.
    - Fix gated on **D21** (DECISIONS_PENDING): ride-the-displayed-surface [Fable rec] vs stay-physical vs other — presentation-layer design, user-visible, Vixy's.
