The RA/DE analog of the azimuth divergence B9 fixed: `ModularObject::getRaDeValue`/`getInfoString`/`getShortInfoNavString` read `Camera::observedPosToRaDe(body->getObservedPosition())`, which disagrees with the old path's `Body::getRaDeValue` (`Utility::rectToSphe(getEarthEquPos)`). Measured on the same frame [harness/artifacts/b9_postfix/*.navstr]: Moon **old `RA 04h57m15s / +26°41'47"`** vs **new `03h44m18s / +14°40'05"`** (~18° in RA, ~12° in DE) — NOT a small residual, a frame/convention delta (candidate: J2000 vs of-date, or an observer-frame rotation). Feeds `getSelectedRA/DE` and the SA/GHA/LHA nav fields (which are all derived from the RA). **Invisible today for the same reason as B9**: ModularObject is uninstantiated in production (§11.60(g)), so `getSelectedRA/DE` currently uses the old `Body`. NOT fixed here — B9's DoD is azimuth only, and the single-authority discipline forbids improvising a second convention. Needs its own probe (does the delta hold clean across bodies/states, like az did?) then a single conversion authority in the same idiom as `ModularObject::altAz()`. A natural next-task companion to B9.
