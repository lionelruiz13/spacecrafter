    - **(d) THE ONE POINT WHERE EXACT REPRODUCTION IS IMPOSSIBLE, AND IT IS NOT A FEEL ITEM — IT IS float32, MEASURED AND BOUNDED.** `[measured: scratch program reproducing lookTo's snap in the camera's own float32 arithmetic; then live, artifacts/f25/postF]` Old's pole clamp pins the VIEW altitude at `π/2 − 1e-6` (T14). `Camera::lookRel` used to reach its parameters through `lookTo(alt, az, 0)`, i.e. params → direction → params, which is the identity in exact arithmetic (D8) and **is not in float32 near a pole**: `sinf(π/2 − 1e-6)` rounds to exactly `1.0f`, so `asinf` gives back exactly `π/2` — the whole clamp epsilon is eaten (measured Δ **−9.5367e-07 rad**) — and once the parameter IS the pole, `cosf` of the float32 `π/2` is **−4.371139e-08**, i.e. NEGATIVE, so the recovered azimuth flips by **exactly π every frame** (measured: 0.700000 → −2.441593 → 0.700000), which `viewRotation()` composes into the roll as a **180° image roll per frame**. The threshold at which a clamp epsilon survives the round trip is `ε > 2^-11.5 = 3.45e-4` rad (derived, then measured: 1e-6/1e-5/1e-4 all collapse to the pole, 3.45e-4 survives) — **345× old's epsilon**. Two ways out: choose a larger epsilon (a number nobody can justify, and a user-visible stopping altitude ⇒ a Vixy feel item) or **do not round-trip**. The snap now ASSIGNS the parameters, which is the as-if identity and strictly more exact, so **old's own epsilon is kept and the reproduction stays exact in form**. What remains is a quantization, and it is PREDICTED rather than tolerated: old pins at the double `1.5707953267948966`, the camera at `float(float(π/2) − float(1e-6))` = `1.5707954168319702`, i.e. **9.0037e-08 rad apart by construction** — measured on the live app after 864 up-steps: **8.997050e-08**, which is **5.16e-06 degrees, 5.9e-05 px** on a 1024-px-radius 180° dome. **No Vixy feel item is owed by the turn ramp**: every term of (b) has an exact camera equivalent, and the one term that could not be reproduced through the existing primitive was reproduced by removing the primitive's own loss.
