# INTENT §11.19c

19c. **Camera view subsystem rework (2026-07-12)** [vixy mandates: remove the half-disabled Rotator ("an attempt to patch and understand"); land the deduce-identical-view correction; smoothing with constant minimal acceleration (dome comfort); equatorial mount as the second complementary viewing system]. All landed; every 19b free-mode inconsistency dissolved with the machinery that carried it.
    - **Rotator removed.** Transitions (setFreeMode / setBoundToSurface / setMount / switchToBody) all use ONE primitive: `recoverParams(R)` deduces the (heading, alt, az) reproducing the total view rotation under the new decomposition — ZXZ Euler extraction of R·placement⁻¹·fold⁻¹, validated standalone (50k random rotations, worst 2.8e-7, pole cases via keep-az policy), and validated in production by setMount (runs at every equator-config launch).
    - **Smoothing**: lookTo/tracking/setHeading ride a two-phase constant-|a| profile (accel to t1, decel to T, zero end velocity), velocity-continuous across retargets (tracking re-plans every frame), on the great circle of the forward direction with alt/az re-derived from the smoothed direction (mount roll follows naturally — the dome-comfort intent). Closed-form solver validated standalone (20k random (v0,d,T): exact landing, minimal-|a| root; the §11.18 parametrization-slip class avoided by re-derivation). Measured live: 1.70 rad swing eases over T=5·Θ/π≈2.7 s with the triangular velocity signature and lands exactly (rect r: 0.99→0.78→0.42→0.01→0.000). isMaxDuration keeps the old Rotator angle-scaling semantics; duration≤0 snaps; `set heading` now honors its duration through the coreLink seam (ms→s).
    - **EQUATORIAL mount** (config `viewing_mode`, same key as old): fold X(π/2−lat) in the view composition — the placement latitude cancels algebraically, longitude acts as hour-angle, boundToSurface co-rotation becomes RA-tracking; the two mounts are the same family around a different trust axis [vixy]. Old-mount semantics preserved: the view stays LOCAL-frame locked under observer moves (foldLat interception in update(); measured before it: a 5.55° moveto lat change pitched the sky by exactly that — old sky-locking is flag_lock_equ_pos, a separate unported feature).
    - **Hint draw-order** [vixy caught the symptom: the Moon's hint drew in FRONT of its disc]: DrawHelper's pushCommand positions the segment containing content queued since the PREVIOUS boundary; with per-body boundaries the hint (queued after clearDepth) landed after the disc. Fixed: farComponents draw BEFORE clearDepth + helper->nextDraw before nextCommandBuffer — hint behind its own disc, in front of farther bodies (per-body depth granularity: closer to the PipelineFamily contract than old's all-hints-behind-all-discs). This also closes the "Moon-box animation noise" mis-attribution in 19a.
    - **Final measurements**: equator config (shipped) tracked AND fresh-start: max 0.09 px, view terms 0.0000°; horizon: unchanged (0.09 px); scenes A–D re-run green (P1 exact, P2 ≤1e-7, P4 11–20 km), view terms ≤ 0.0235° (residual = old's asymptotic ease tail, z-only). drive_scenes.py settle waits bumped 3→15 s (they were calibrated for snap tracking).
    - **Remaining open**: sky-lock (flag_lock_equ_pos analog) unported; ~~free-mode transitions runtime-unexercised~~ exercised at scale since §11.36 (scene E: freeMode entry/exit, in-freeMode reference switches across five shells, both directions ×2 — all green); zoom-fov and zoom_offset seams (19a) unchanged; position moves (moveRel) still linear-interpolated, not min-accel — extend the law there if dome comfort demands it.
