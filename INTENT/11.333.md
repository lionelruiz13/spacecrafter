## (d) The record - form decided at the anchor (I9), channels walked from it

```
#	screen_record	1
#	B	engine(o|n)	name	x	y	size            x,y: Vulkan axes, +x right, +y down, 1 = the viewport radius; size: angular radius / half-fov of that engine (1 = the viewport radius)
#	F	frame	dt_ms	jd	drawn(o|n)	fov_old_deg	half_fov_new_rad    the F line CLOSES its frame: the B lines above it, since the previous F line, are that frame's
#	viewport	projector	<cx>	<cy>	<radius>	renderer	<cx>	<cy>	<radius>	scissor	<ox>	<oy>	<w>	<h>
B	n	Saturn	0.123456789	-0.0123456789	0.00123456789
B	o	Saturn	...
F	0	7	2461233.5	o	60	0.52359879
```
Tab-separated; `#` lines are the header; numbers at 9 significant digits for the float members, 17 for the doubles (jd, the fovs); the frame counter restarts at 0 at every `state on`; `dt_ms` is the frame's `delta_time` as the engine consumed it (the prefix sum is the engine's own time base - the exploitation aligns two runs on it, never on wall clock); both fovs in their engine's own units (the seam ring's convention).
