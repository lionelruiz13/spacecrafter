    - **(b) M1 — DECLARATION SURVIVAL: 95 % OF EVERYTHING EVER DECLARED IS STILL THERE, AND EVERY REMOVAL HAPPENED WITHIN 127 DAYS OF THE DECLARATION'S BIRTH** [measured: every `*.hpp` of the library at each of the 325 commits, a declaration keyed (header, function name), overloads merged, operators excluded].
