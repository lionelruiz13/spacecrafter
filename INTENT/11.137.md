   it (§11.117) for exactly the reason §5.46 records: the up-chain never wrote the frame, so

   written from the same `flat`, on adjacent lines, at every site) — which is stated at the
   member so the next reader inherits the argument rather than the puzzle.
2. **`TrailModule::accumulate` silently DROPS samples when a step lands a hair short.**
   `[measured: harness/artifacts/f29/pre, 1.0-day stepping]` `dt = |(date - lastJD) /
   deltaTrail|` truncated to `int`, with `date` = the LIGHT-RETARDED jd, so a commanded

   elapsed time keeps growing so a sample is only late, not lost; under date-stepping (every
   scripted show that jumps dates) it is lost. → **§5.75**.
3. **No planet's orbit line reaches the frame from a moon's surface** (§ (c)(1)): the orbit
   depth bucket is the union of the notable bodies' slices, and from the Moon that is the

## (l) DEVIATIONS from the dispatch, each with its argument

1. **The fix is two sites, not the one the row records.** The row's shape was followed
   exactly; the source enumeration found a second site with the identical shape and an
   independent reach (the invisible camera reference, §5.32's own branch). The mandate says

2. **The render half is measured on the TRAIL, not the ORBIT**, and at fov 140 as well as
   340. Both moves are forced by MEASURED renderer behaviour (§ (c)), not by convenience:
   the orbit line does not reach the frame at all from the subject scene, and the wide-field
   cull removes the very vertex the prediction is about. The three consumers share one
   expression, so the trail's verdict is the orbit's and the tail's.
3. **The P3 gate is the closest approach, not the head pixel.** The head pixel carries an
   `argmax` raster-order tie whose band is ~74 px wide on the post-fix geometry — a known
   systematic of the instrument, not of the subject. Both numbers are reported and they
   agree; the gate uses the one without the systematic.
4. **P2 is a ratio test, not bit equality.** Frames run between the freeze-leg dump and the
   switch, so bit equality would have been an over-strong claim; the ratio (2.85e-07 pre,
   1.0 post) says the same thing honestly and quantifies the residual as 52 km of
   retardation jitter out of 1.24 AU.
5. **The prediction file was edited after its first commit**, twice, each time appending a
   paragraph (P3', P3c, P3'') that records a MEASUREMENT which moved WHERE the prediction is
   read, never WHAT it says. The original P1/P2/P3/P4 text is unchanged in the file and in
   `5df7d17`, and P1/P2 were already confirmed on the first pre-fix run before either edit.
