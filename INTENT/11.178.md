## (a) What the two schemas are, and how they were made into a ruler

§11.169(b) gives, from Vixy's transmission: the **error schema** — WHAT · CONSEQUENCES ·
PREVENTION; the **acting-default schema** (D12) — CAUSE · CONTENT · OVERRIDE; and the
**self-containment criterion** binding the action element of both (*"a field set to an
invalid value must enumerate the valid values, or their SHAPE if not enumerable"*), with
the scope floor *"at least for user-facing error like scripts or datas"*.

Two clauses of (b) shaped the method and are worth naming because they change the count.
*"One log entry carrying all elements satisfies both"* ⇒ the unit scored is the **emission
event**, not the source line: where one refusal produces a fixed pair of lines from one
chokepoint (`Could not execute: <commandline>` at `app_command_interface.cpp:1173` plus
the message at `:1174`), the pair is one record and the echoed command line counts toward
WHAT. And the **double face** ⇒ a site can be scored on both triples at once; 17 sites
are.

The element tests were written down BEFORE the classification and committed with it
(`claude/harness/f58_gaptable.py`, docstring): Y / P (partial) / N, per element, with the
discriminating question stated for each — e.g. WHAT is N not only when the record names
nothing but when it **misidentifies which element is at fault**, and CONTENT is N when the
stated content is **wrong**, because a record that misstates its own decision is worse
than silence: it is a false belief with a citation.

## (i) Limits — what this audit does not cover, stated so the next reader is not misled

1. **Silent sites are not exhaustively enumerated** and cannot be by this method. 8 are
   recorded; a complete census needs a per-branch read of ~60 handlers and 13 loaders.
2. **The universe is 22 files; 20 more are inventoried and unclassified** (274 sites).
   The exclusion is the mandate's five-family boundary, not a judgement that those
   loaders are cleaner.
3. **No launch was made.** Every verdict is a reading of source at `d6aec251`. What a
   record LOOKS like in a real log — interleaving, the composition of the pair, whether
   the `(Debug): ` tag reads as noise to an actual operator — is unmeasured, and the
   §5.117 row's consequence is derived, not observed. One launch with a deliberately bad
   script would measure it.
4. **The element verdicts are one reader's**, applied by stated tests. The tests are
   committed; a second reader disagreeing on a row changes a cell, and the totals are
   robust to a handful of cells because the distribution is not close to any boundary
   (CONSEQUENCES 20 of 288 does not become a different conclusion at 30).
5. **Translation is not scored.** `_()` wraps some messages and not others, and
   `app_command_interface.cpp:3848/:3879` wrap FRENCH literals in an otherwise-English
   channel, so the translation table is keyed on French at those two sites and on English
   everywhere else. Noted on the rows, outside the schemas.
