    - **(b) The class fix, and it is a mechanism rather than two patches [code `83324455`].** `Context` — the owner of every manager in question — now takes teardown hooks and runs them at the START of `~Context`, in reverse registration order, in the window `Renderer::releaseRegistry()` and `ShadowService::release()` already use (`context.hpp` `onManagerTeardown`, `context.cpp`). That is I3 stated at the one place that knows: the owner of the state notifies its dependents, instead of each dependent guessing when its manager dies. Registering is also what makes a non-owning manager reference legal under I5 — lifetime-guaranteed or destruction-notified, and this is the notification. The instances then fall out: **§5.55** is `AxisModule.cpp` registering one hook that resets `uColor`, with the comment naming why the other three members need nothing; **§5.57** is `s_texture::forceUnload()` moving out of `main()` into `~Context`, as the **terminal** drain — placed AFTER `releaseRegistry()` on purpose, because that call itself feeds the ring (it drops the pointer and sun-halo textures) — and the call site in `main.cpp:375` is DELETED rather than kept as a safety net, because two authorities for one drain is the next silent desync (I2). `forceUnload` itself gained the five containers the audit found and an order comment stating the rule it now obeys.

    - **(h) NEW §5.61 — a lost wakeup in the queue the frame pacing rides [observed, at source; not fixed, EntityCore is read-only for this task].** `WorkQueue::pop` (`EntityCore/Tools/SafeQueue.hpp:174-187`) tests `count` and then calls `cv.wait(lock)`; producers (`:143-162`) never take the mutex and notify only on the 0→1 transition. A push landing between the consumer's test and its wait is therefore a lost wakeup, and every subsequent push is silent because `count != 0`. `DrawHelper::waitFrame` flushes ONCE before its untimed wait (`draw_helper.cpp:409`), so a lost wakeup there parks both threads for good — a mechanism that would produce §5.59's stack exactly. It is *recorded, not claimed*: no measurement attributes any particular hang to it. It also explains why `waitAllFrames` uses `queue.waitIdle()` (`SafeQueue.hpp:232-241`), which re-notifies every 100 µs until the queue is drained and therefore heals the class, rather than a single `flush()`.
