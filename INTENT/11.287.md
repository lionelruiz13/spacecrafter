So the engine-first reflex is not a preference for engines; it is the three generators meeting at one object. The delta from usual practice is real [knowledge]: most either adopt an engine whole (its model held in the head) or write the application directly (each piece coordinating itself); the coordination layer built first and small is the rare shape.
