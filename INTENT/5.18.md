Original entry (user-reported [vixy 2026-07-20: "an issue some users genuinely had and reported"]; rode B10, §5.2): Mechanism: the interactive movers are multiplicative on the proximity factor, which is zero at height 0 — `multAlt` [observed: Camera.hpp:99-105, `coef = distanceToReference()*(coef-1)`] and, in free mode, `moveRelLon`/`moveRelLat` [observed: Camera.hpp:74-89, `*= distanceToReference() * 5.f`]. Reachable two ways: a script/config setting height exactly 0, and repeated descent multiplying the factor down to float underflow. **Not fixed by the datum/ground split alone** (with the defaults, height 0 sits exactly at `ground_radius` ⇒ factor still 0): it needs an explicit floor in the shared proximity-factor authority.
