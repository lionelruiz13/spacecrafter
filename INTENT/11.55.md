    - **(e) The reversible pair, both entries, and the case that was supposed to break it.** Three reloads in one uninterrupted session, the second starting from the state the first produced (mutated data), the third from the state the second produced (restored data) — plus a second run with one reload and a third with two. **The camera's reference body was Earth every time — i.e. a body the reload destroys and recreates**, which is the `ModularBodyPtr`/I5 case the row was aimed at.     - **(f) THE DEFECT THE ROW UNCOVERED — a cross-frame non-owning body cache with neither lifetime guarantee nor destruction notification (I5), fixed at the class.** `EnvironmentManager` keeps `activeChain` (raw `ModularBody*`, the previous frame's reference chain) as the diff base for enter/leave edges, and dereferences every entry absent from the new chain to fire `leave()` [observed: EnvironmentManager.cpp:31-37]; `lastReference` is a second, compare-only raw pointer [observed: :136]. Any body destroyed **between two frames** leaves a freed pointer in that base — a system reload does it to the whole chain at once, and `body action drop` on any chain ancestor is the same bug with a rarer trigger. Fix: `ModularBody::~ModularBody` **pushes** the destruction (`EnvironmentManager::notifyBodyDestroyed`, I3 — the owner notifies, the dependent never polls), which drops the entry (a destroyed body's members die with it, so *dropping* is the correct edge: there is nothing left to `leave()`) and nulls `lastReference` (a recycled address would otherwise read as "same reference" and swallow the landscape/atmosphere-model edge). This is I5's own remedy clause applied — a non-owning reference is legal *if* destruction-notified — not a guard at the reload site. Sweep: `activeChain`/`lastReference` are the **only** cross-frame non-owning `ModularBody*` holders [measured: grep over `src/experimentalModule/*.hpp` + `ssystem_factory.hpp` — the others are `ModularBodyPtr` (redirected), `lastFit`/`selectedBody` (nulled in the dtor), `notableBody` (per-frame), `sortedSystemBodies` (dtor-deregistered)].
