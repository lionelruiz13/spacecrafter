**D34 — cross-session identity key for the override ledger (and for non-body selections).** `[B31; A29 §5.5, A30(iii) §11.49(b), §11.109(c)]`

The same question at a different scale governs **non-body selections** (a saved star/nebula selection needs a catalogue identifier) — folded in here so the two cannot diverge.

**D36 — is a running SHOW part of the session?** `[B31; §11.66, §2.0 D8, §2(b)]`
