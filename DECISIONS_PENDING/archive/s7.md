The design is regime-partitioned (Renderer.hpp's own words, verified): *Outer* = depth buffer SHARED with grounded bodies (today's merged bucket — implemented and, after §5.29/§5.30/§5.33, measured correct end-to-end); *Surface* = buffer SPLIT per grounded body, parent's depth trace prefilled. (ii) in Surface, does the prefill wait for row 16/D4, or should the ray row extend below 2·scaledRadius (the §11.97(e)/§11.100(g)(ii) hole) so a terrain trace exists to prefill with?
