**D22 — B14 90° meridian correction: apply?** `[= ledger A33; §11.101(b), §5.28, §11.86]`
The `rot_pole_w0` conversion (B14-W0, `ModularSystem.cpp:852-858`) lands the IAU prime meridian on texture u 0.75 (mesh x̂) while the shipped texture convention is u 0.5 (image centre — anchored independently by Earth/Greenwich, and by the planet offsets: Saturn 0.5000, Mercury 0.4998, Mars 0.4978, Neptune 0.4923). Consequence: **Iapetus, Amalthea and Proteus render 90° off** (measured: `iapetus.png` dark-centroid u 0.224 vs 0.25 predicted); the other 17 landed moons carry placeholder textures and are visually unaffected. Root: the `+π/2` fudge in `getAxisRotation()` (`ModularBody.hpp:409`, its own `// TODO Fix ojml ?`) compensates the mesh's −0.25 texcoord; the conversion reasoned about the matrix and inherited the fudge without its compensation. The fetched W₀ VALUES and the cited-fetch discipline are NOT in question — only the axis they are converted onto.
`[Fable rec]` fix in the CONVERSION (one authority, code layer), NOT in the 20 bodies' data — but this changes rendered output on shipped bodies, so it is a D9/parity decision, not an implementation step. (ii) retire the `+π/2` fudge at the mesh, or keep it and correct the conversion? (iii) the planet rows stay untouched either way (already consistent). Cheapest confirmation before deciding: add `rot_pole_w0` to Mercury — the surface must visibly rotate 90°.
