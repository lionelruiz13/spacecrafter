3. THE DECISIVE TEST - IS THERE AN ALPHA-INDEPENDENT EMISSION AT ALL?
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 P3  ARM A4 (G4 on: drawStarProxy suppressed while inBand) measures the
     INTERIOR ALONE across the band, so the dot's own brightening and the
     (1-t) mixing both leave the measurement. SS11.82(a) used exactly this
     instrument (env-gated dot-suppression, throwaway) to attribute the
     pre-fix floor C ~ 43167.
     PREDICTION FROM THE CODE: the interior emits EXACTLY ZERO at
     drawAlpha = 0 -
       * the big halo passes `getHaloColor() * ModularBody::drawAlpha` and
         sun_big_halo.frag:36 is `FragColor = color * (farHalo*max(1,cmag+0.1)
         + nearHalo)` - colour multiplies BOTH components, so colour 0 emits 0
         under BLEND_ADD;
       * every drawHaloCore halo passes `color * cmag` with cmag *= drawAlpha
         (:1961), and the `rmag < 1.21f && cmag < 0.05f` skip rule (:1962)
         drops the sub-pixel ones outright;
       * no non-halo channel is reached: every body in the band is halo-only
         (largest disc 3.5e-3 px << the 0.008 screenSize mesh threshold),
         drawNested calls drawSystemBodies (no trails/orbits/pointer pass),
         and flag_experimental_oort is off in the field.
     So: fit addI_interior(t) = F + G*t over the LOW-t half (t = 0.0525,
     0.1516, 0.2285, 0.3283, where the metric's clipping is weakest).
     PREDICTED  F in [-400, +400]  (zero within the instrument's floor)
     and G ~ 60000 .. 100000.
     REFUTED IF F > +800 (2% of the swing): something in the interior is then
     alpha-independent, the mandate's (4) applies, and A3 vs A12 says which
     floor owns it.
