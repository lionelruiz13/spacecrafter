===============================================================================

POSITIVE CONTROL, run before the rate is believed.  The reader must be shown
to SEE a non-finite value: a synthetic dump line carrying `"nan"` for
`new.scaling` is fed to the same predicate and must be classified as a FIRE.
A criterion that cannot report a fire cannot report 0/6 either.  If the
control does not fire, the rate is withheld.

first 6 cold launches of F12 and then 0 of 4 vs 0 of 4 in a controlled

more sample of a startup race, and the row stays OPEN either way (its fix is
EntityCore, the owner's).

(which would widen the row's scope), or the positive control failing.
