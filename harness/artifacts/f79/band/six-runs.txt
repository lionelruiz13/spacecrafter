So the 2048^2 readback of this scene is bit-reproducible across a compositor change
AND five days of engine commits.  What that buys: the photometric arm's "in band"
is not the only available discriminator on this scene -- byte identity holds, so a
future stack question can be asked at 32 bits per pixel rather than at +-1.0 on a
mean.  What it does NOT buy: the md5 stays RECORDED, NEVER GATED (11.176(a)) -- a
legitimate texture-cache or driver change may move bytes without moving luminance,
and gating it would convert a stronger instrument into a more brittle one.
