      every k in 2..10 (5 * 44 = 220 individual inode equalities).

      window drops: 22428043/22429251/22429311/22429312/22428144 appear at index

  (b) THE PID LINE -- INTERNAL channel, content.  main.cpp:239 writes
      `My getpid() is <pid>` once per launch.  [P] spacecrafter.j.log contains
      the pid of launch k-j, for j = 1..min(k-1,7), and the driver knows every
      pid because it spawned them (subprocess.Popen.pid).
  (c) A PER-LAUNCH TOKEN -- SCRIPT channel, content.  The driver sends

7. THE COST (D11)                                                      [P]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The rotation is O(1) syscalls per channel at open and ZERO per frame: nothing
in the change is reachable from the frame loop (cLog::write is untouched).  Per
channel at open: 1 is_regular_file + <= 1 file_size + <= 1 remove + 7 rename
attempts + 7 is_regular_file (the count) + ONE directory scan of log/ for the
legacy pile (432 entries here).  The directory scan is the dominant term and it
is the reason to predict a number rather than "negligible":

  [P] total added open-time cost, five channels + the report, on the real home
      with a warm dentry cache: < 25 ms, and > 0.5 ms (432 dirents scanned five
      times).  Measured once with a throwaway timing build (SDL_GetPerformance-
      Counter around the five openLog calls in main.cpp), which is then
      reverted; against D11's 1 ms/frame denominator this is a one-off at
      startup, not a per-frame cost.
  [P] 0 added syscalls and 0 added instructions per frame.
