1. THE MODEL, AND THE TWO MUTATIONS COMMITTED BESIDE IT
================================================================================
  viewMat()            = R' . Rv . T(...) . placement      [Camera.cpp:184-197]
  getObservedPosition()  is a per-frame copy of that action, so an observed
                         position carries  R' . Rv . d     [Camera.cpp:157-172]
  observedToLocalPos   = Rv^T . o                          [Camera.hpp:260-262]
  =>  the readout direction is  C . d,  C = Rv^T . R' . Rv

  R'  = xrotation(effectiveViewOffset * ModularBody::halfFov)  [Camera.cpp:139-145]
  Rv  = zrotation(heading+pi).xrotation(pi/2-alt).zrotation(az-pi/2).fold()
                                                                [Camera.cpp:93-104]
  fold() = xrotation(pi/2 - foldLat)  (EQUATORIAL mount, anchored) [Camera.cpp:86-91]
  halfFov = fov*pi/360, so halfFov = pi/2 at the shipped fov 180
                                              [ModularBody.hpp:1868, projector.cpp:340]

MUTATION M1 -- the rotation the other way:      C1 = Rv^T . R'^T . Rv
MUTATION M2 -- the conjugation the other way:   C2 = Rv  . R'  . Rv^T

P1 [OFFLINE, MEASURED BEFORE THE LEG -- f96_frame.cpp, artifacts/f96/f96_frame_pre.txt]
   C is a rotation by exactly |offset|*halfFov about Rv^T*x_eye, whatever the
   camera state: over 243 states x 3 offsets the probe reads
     max |angle(C) - |offset|*halfFov| = 0.000084852 deg   (float32 residual)
     max angle(axis(C), Rv^T*x)        = 0.000014514 deg
   THE SHIPPED CELL (fov 180, `set zoom_offset 0.3`): angle(C) = 27.000021918 deg
   -- i.e. 0.3 x 90 deg = 27 deg, to float32.
   The same probe reads, on an offset-carrying observed position over 64
   directions: SHIPPED (Rv^T) max error 44.999997 deg (that is the o = -0.5 cell,
   0.5 x 90); THE FIX ((R'.Rv)^T) max error 0.000014 deg.
   The mutations are DISTINGUISHABLE: max sep(C.d, C1.d) = 89.999985 deg,
   max sep(C.d, C2.d) = 89.751869 deg (min over states 0.084084 deg).
