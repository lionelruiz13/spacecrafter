1.  THE STRUCTURAL CLAIM THE WHOLE PREDICTION RESTS ON  (P0, checkable offline)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
At a PINNED clock the mean anomaly M handed to a solver is the same at every
call, so each call applies the SAME map f to the seed.  Taking two steps per
call therefore does not change the iterate sequence at all -- it changes which
element of it a call returns:

        post-fix state after k calls  ==  pre-fix state after 2k calls,
        bit for bit, same arithmetic, same order.

P0 PREDICTION: the post-fix harness's `--converge E N` value at call k is
BYTE-IDENTICAL (printed %.17g) to the pre-fix harness's at call 2k, for every
eccentricity tested.  If this fails, the loop was not placed around the step.

Two consequences used below:
  P0a  a PARKED body's one use (1 + RESUME_EXTRA_ITERATIONS = 5 calls) lands on
       the SAME double the pre binary reaches at 10 calls.  11.220(j1) measured
       the pre binary at 9 calls -> 1.09669e-05 deg and at 15 -> 2.20333e-05
       deg, so 10 lies inside the band the corpus floor already occupies.
  P0b  a WALKED body is evaluated ~2225 times before the launch-state dump
       (Mars evalCount at P0, parked_table_prepost.txt), so pre sits at f^2225
       and post at f^4450 of the same sequence.  Both are deep inside the map's
       double-precision attractor.  If that attractor is a FIXED point the two
       are the same double; if it is a 2-cycle they differ by the cycle's own
       amplitude, which 11.215(g) measured on this corpus at <= 9.437e-16 AU
       (136 ulps) -- 4 orders below the F91 alt/az floor.
