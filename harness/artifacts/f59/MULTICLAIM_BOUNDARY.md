# F59 scope 3 — the multi-claim corpus and rule, committed BEFORE the walk

§11.165(h)(3)(ii), the named-not-run case: *"a superseder that corrects TWO claims of a
target where only one is marked (half-2 answers per PAIR, not per claim)"*. A per-pair
test is structurally blind to it: one covered claim makes the pair MARKED forever.

## The rule the walk applies, per pair

1. Read the superseder's clauses that name the target. Enumerate the **distinct claims OF
   THE TARGET** it corrects, supersedes or refutes — C1…Cn. A claim is distinct when the
   target could have been right about one and wrong about the other.
2. Read the marker(s) at the target.
3. Score each Ci **COVERED** (the marker states it), **PARTIAL** (the marker points at the
   superseder but does not carry this claim's content), **UNCOVERED** (absent).
4. A pair is **MULTI-CLAIM-COMPLETE** iff every Ci is COVERED. Anything else is named, with
   the uncovered claim quoted.

Enrichments, escalations, confirmations and mints are **not** claims for this test —
§11.113(p) binds supersession/refutation/correction. Where a superseder adds something the
target did not know, that is recorded as a note, never scored as a gap (the §5.51/§11.125
distinction made at scope 2, finding M6).

enough to walk whole, which is strictly better than any sampling rule, so none is adopted.

## The uncovered remainder, stated

Every OTHER marked pair in the §11↔§11 corpus is **not** walked — the ~20 direction-inverted
markers §11.165(c) enumerates, plus every marked pair among the scan's 150 that neither F49
placed nor this audit surfaced. That remainder is the honest boundary of scope 3, and it is
the natural corpus for a later pass. **Sizing it, so the remainder is a number and not a
gesture:** the frozen scan reports 150 candidate pairs of which 88 are mechanically
unmarked, i.e. **62 mechanically-marked pairs** exist corpus-wide; this walk covers 23, of
which 6 (corpus A′ + B, minus overlap) lie in the §11↔§11 half. The remainder is therefore
roughly 40–55 pairs, none of them §5-targeted.
