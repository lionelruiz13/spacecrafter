F111 / P5 -- THE DRAWN ORBIT LINE: WHAT THE MODEL SAYS, AND WHY THE SCREENSHOT
              COULD NOT BE MADE TO SAY IT
===============================================================================

THE CLAIM (prediction.txt P5, committed before the build).  The pre-fix line's
FIRST sampled point is computed from `iterativeLastE` as the frame left it -- at
the body's own date -- while the point asks for date - 90*increment, i.e. HALF a
visualisation period away, and it gets two Newton steps.  So the pre-fix line's
first points are wrong by the same order as the transient, and the post-fix line
is the converged one (which is also the OLD path's line, same algorithm):

  body        max|pre-post|  at_d   pre_vs_exact   post_vs_exact
  Neso           0.58723 AU    1       0.58723 AU     4.18e-07 AU
  Setebos        0.23454       0       0.23454        5.03e-08
  Sycorax        0.16316       5       0.16316        1.62e-08
  Laomedeia      0.11449       0       0.11449        5.39e-09

THE INSTRUMENT, FIVE CONFIGURATIONS, AND WHAT EACH MEASURED.  All frames
2048x2048, compared as (count of differing pixels, max absolute channel delta).

 1. DEFAULT camera, shipped scene, PRE vs POST with the flag ON:
        92590 differing, max 89
    A/A floor from two launches of the SAME binary, same flag state:
        PRE vs PRE   91533   POST vs POST   91689
    and the flag OFF vs ON difference INSIDE one launch: 91505.
    => the floor is the whole of the signal; nothing can be read here.

 2. QUIET scene (stars, constellations, milky way, nebulae, atmosphere,
    landscape, grids, hints, trails, meteors all off), camera tracking
    Jupiter, fov 2 deg, selection pointer ON:
        A/A flag OFF 975, A/A flag ON 820, flag OFF vs ON 748, max 232
    => the pointer ANIMATES; the A/A floor still exceeds the signal.

 3. Same, pointer OFF, tracking RELEASED after the aim:
        A/A flag OFF 0, A/A flag ON 0, flag OFF vs ON 0,
        non-black pixels in the whole frame 210
    => the aim drifted back; nothing of the Jupiter system is on screen.

 4. Same, pointer OFF, tracking KEPT ON, fov 2:
        A/A flag OFF 449, A/A flag ON 366, flag OFF vs ON 375, max 6
    => the lines reach a MAX CHANNEL DELTA OF SIX at the shipped
       `planet_orbits_color = 0.2,0.2,0.2`; the floor (449) still wins.

 5. Same, with `planet_orbits_color = 1.0,1.0,1.0` written into the FARM COPY of
    config.ini only (the real ~/.spacecrafter md5-asserted in == out, and the
    change is identical in both arms so it cannot bias a pre/post comparison):
        A/A flag ON 0 (BYTE-IDENTICAL frames -- the floor is finally gone)
        but flag OFF vs ON 0 as well, and at the DEFAULT camera in the same
        quiet scene all four frames are byte-identical.
    => the floor was removed and the SIGNAL turned out to be zero: no satellite
       orbit line reaches the frame at either camera in that scene.

CONCLUSION, stated as a limit and not as a result.  The screenshot channel is
NOT a discriminating instrument for this claim on this scene set: either its A/A
floor exceeds the predicted signal, or the signal is absent.  The line claim
therefore rests on the model (P5), which is labelled as such.  A body-space
readout of `orbitPoint[]` -- which does not exist: the dump's `modules` key
carries only the module NAMES -- is what would settle it, and it is recorded as
owed rather than improvised.

A SECOND FINDING, ABOUT AN EXISTING LEDGER NUMBER.  Sec.11.229(f2) reads *"with
`flag planets_orbits on` the frame changes by 91774 pixels (the lines are
drawn)"*, and its sibling leg recorded 91563 for the satellites flag
(artifacts/f107/orbitflag_{planets,sat}.json).  Measured today on the same
scene: two launches of ONE binary, same flag state, differ by 91533 pixels.  So
those two numbers sit AT the A/A floor of their own scene and cannot support the
parenthetical -- the differing pixels are the star field, which animates
(twinkle) between any two frames.  The claim they were offered for ("the lines
are drawn") may well be true, but that measurement does not show it.  Recorded
at Sec.11.229(f2) as an annotation, not as a refutation of (f2)'s substance: its
load-bearing result there is the 0-of-120 record comparison, which is
independent of the pixel count and which BOTH binaries reproduce today
(artifacts/f111/europa_specials.txt).
