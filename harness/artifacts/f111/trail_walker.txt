DISPOSITION: A RIDER, NOT THIS TASK.  Three reasons, in order of weight.

1. MAGNITUDE.  The worst record is Elara at 8.92e-05 AU of 3D displacement
   (6.67e-06 AU radial), 3.9 orders below the sampler's worst (0.645 AU on Neso
   from the same model, confirmed in the engine at 0.335 AU) and 2.5 below
   Pasiphae's.  At Elara's ~5.5 AU that is 3.3 arcsec, so it is NOT below
   readout -- which is why it is recorded rather than dismissed.

2. FREQUENCY AND BOUND.  The sampler fires once per vis/180 of simulation time,
   for every shown line, forever.  `resumeAfterHidden` fires once per UNHIDE
   EDGE, and its staleness is bounded by the module's own cadence (`deltaTrail`,
   one simulation day, TrailModule.hpp:193) because `missed` is the floor of
   (date - lastJD)/deltaTrail (TrailModule.cpp:190) and each reconstructed
   sample is evaluated 1 + RESUME_EXTRA_ITERATIONS = 5 times AT ITS OWN DATE
   (:229).  The sampler's staleness is half a VISUALISATION PERIOD and is not
   bounded by anything the module owns.

3. THE FIX IS NOT THE SAME FIX, and this is the decisive one.  F111's fix
   RESTORES a shape the old path already has: the batch pair with its own seed,
   in the tree, called from the old plot, with the requirement it encodes
   readable at its own site.  The trail walker has NO old-path counterpart --
   trail reconstruction across a hide is new-path-only (D23 clause iv,
   Sec.11.113(b)(iv)) -- so there is no shape to restore and any change is a
   DESIGN CHOICE among at least three: re-converge the body after the walk;
   give the walker its own seed (a third `mutable` member, i.e. the same
   question the batch seed answered, asked again); or evaluate the
   reconstructed samples through the batch pair (which would be the closest
   analogue, and which would silently change what a trail point MEANS for a
   body whose orbit type has no batch seed).  Choosing among those is not
   decision-free and is not this task's (Sec.11.161(b) stratum: the
   experimentalModule is the owner's).

ROUTED, NOT MINTED.  Whether 8.92e-05 AU / 3.3 arcsec on one frame after one
unhide meets Sec.5.79's "with consequence" is a MINT decision for the supervisor.
The reach is `flag object_trails on` plus any hide/unhide of a body with a trail
(the shows do this), so the shipped-surface half of the criterion is met.

WHAT IS NOT MEASURED HERE, said rather than glossed: this is a SLICE
measurement, on the tree's own solver text through f107_model's re-derivation,
not an engine one.  An engine measurement needs a hide/unhide leg with a dump on
the frame after the unhide -- the same one-frame-capture problem this task
solved for the sampler by running the clock, and the trail walker has no
equivalent "every frame" regime because it fires on an edge.  That leg is owed.
