REFUTED, AND WHAT EACH REFUTATION TAUGHT
  P4  "through the shipped `float` parentSideralDay the frozen spin is >= 1 deg
      out on Mars."  MEASURED -0.000007 deg live (t0) and -0.000014 deg 12 h
      later; offline on the corrected parent, -0.000003 deg.  CAUSE: I assumed
      `re.period` is a double.  It is a FLOAT, and so are `offset`,
      `obliquity`, `ascendingNode` and `precessionRate`
      [observed: rotation_elements.hpp:33-38].  getSiderealTime and the frozen
      model therefore read the SAME truncated period and there is no float cost
      to find.  CONSEQUENCE FOR THE ROW: on a linear-`re` parent, S5.21's
      defect (a) is an I2 defect and not a numeric one -- the child holds a
      frozen COPY of the parent's spin state, so a runtime
      `body name <parent> rot_periode ...` no longer reaches it -- while on
      EARTH it stays numeric and large (P5).  The dispatch section had said
      exactly this ("the float cost plus the FROZEN period/phase"); I predicted
      the float cost too high by five orders of magnitude.
  P6  "the new path's observer coincides with old's AnchorPointBody direction
      to <= 1e-5 deg."  MEASURED 2.02e-5 deg max over 64 states.  The claim
      holds; my BOUND was too tight by 2x.  2e-5 deg is the float floor for
      this composition and sits inside the project's own measured alt/az floor
      of 3e-5 deg (S11.213(h2)).
  P9  "get_nutation's un-reset accumulator makes get_apparent_sidereal_time
      non-repeatable by > 0.001 deg."  MEASURED 0.000000000 deg over 21 round
      trips.  The accumulator really is never reset
      [observed: sideral_time.c:216-262, `c_longitude += ...` with no zeroing,
      only `c_ecliptic` re-initialised], but the `c_longitude /= 36000000.`
      that follows the sum divides the CARRY-OVER too, so each recompute keeps
      about 1/3.6e7 of the previous value: a residue of order 1e-10 deg.  The
      code smell is real and its consequence is not.  Recorded because a
      refuted prediction about a defect is worth as much as a confirmed one,
      and because the next reader of that file will see the same smell.

ONE PROBE DEFECT, FOUND BY THE LIVE LEG DISAGREEING WITH THE OFFLINE ONE
  Section (2) of `f97_frame.cpp` first reported 57.750889 deg where the live
  leg reported 86.306371.  Cause: its "Mars" was not Mars.  protosystem.cpp
  turns `rot_pole_ra`/`rot_pole_de` into obliquity/ascendingNode only AFTER
  rotating the J2000 pole into VSOP87 by `mat_j2000_to_vsop87`
  [observed: protosystem.cpp:930-941, navigator.cpp:223-225]; the probe applied
  :940-941 to the RAW J2000 pole.  Section (6) of the probe is the correction,
  and it is a CROSS-CHECK rather than a patch: with the conversion applied the
  offline number is 86.306365 deg against the live 86.306371, and the probe's
  rotEquToVsop87 third column (0.446140676, -0.055631381, 0.893232135) is the
  dumped Mars `rotLocalToParent` r[8..10] digit for digit.  The two
  independently written compositions now agree, which is the thing that says
  they are reading the same object.
