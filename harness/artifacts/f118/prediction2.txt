4.  A SECOND MECHANISM FOUND WHILE MODELLING, RECORDED AND NOT CHASED
=============================================================================

`IterativeEll::operator()` FOLDS the mean anomaly into [0,2pi) every call
(iterative_orbits.hpp:125-127) while its `H` accumulates unfolded.  So on the
one cell per orbit where the fold wraps between the seed's date and the
evaluation's, the seed is a full turn from the root and two Newton steps do not
recover: modelled at Sedna 731.9 AU, Haumea 7.13, Makemake 2.91, Juno 1.18,
Pallas 0.89, Vesta 0.017.  `EllipticalOrbit::eccentricAnomaly` does NOT fold
(orbit.cpp:579-581), so the `ell` family cannot show this.

SAID PLAINLY: this is a property of the SHARED solver, not of the trail walker,
and it does not need a hide at all -- a running clock crosses the same fold.
It is MODELLED, NOT MEASURED, and this task cannot measure it: the wrap window
is `staleness/period` of the orbit (2.2e-07 to 7.5e-04), and a search over every
(missed, staleness) pair the retention bound allows from JD0 -- 60 samples for
the three Asteroid-class bodies, 1460 for the others -- finds NO reachable
combination (f118_predict, the reachability scan, no hit on any of the six).
Recorded for the supervisor as a candidate of its own; not chased here.
